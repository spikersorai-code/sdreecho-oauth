1. Purpose

SdreClip is a personal automation application. It takes recordings of the owner's own Kick live streams, cuts them into short vertical clips and uploads those clips to the owner's own TikTok account. It is not a public service and is not offered to third parties.

2. Authorization

SdreClip only acts within the permissions the account holder explicitly grants on the TikTok authorization screen (user.info.basic, video.publish). Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok account settings, after which the application stops posting.

3. Content and responsibility

All published content is produced from the account holder's own live streams. The account holder is responsible for the content and agrees to use the application in accordance with the TikTok Terms of Service, the TikTok Community Guidelines and applicable law.
