How SdreClip works

From a finished live stream to a published TikTok video, every step is automated and runs on SdreClip's own server.

  1. Stream archive. After a live stream on Kick ends, its recording (VOD) belongs to the Sdre channel and is added to the processing queue.
  2. Highlight selection. Audio peaks and the transcript are analysed to pick the most engaging 20–60 second moments. Moments with offensive language are skipped.
  3. Vertical edit. Each moment is converted to 1080×1920: facecam on top, gameplay below, with word-by-word captions and a short title.
  4. Quality check. Every video is verified for duration, resolution and audio before it can be published.
  5. Owner authorization. The channel owner connects their TikTok account once through TikTok Login Kit and approves the user.info.basic and video.publish permissions. Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok settings.
  6. Publishing. On a fixed schedule, SdreClip uploads the next clip using the Content Posting API Direct Post flow with the caption and privacy level chosen by the owner.
  7. Tracking. The publish status returned by TikTok is recorded so the same clip is never posted twice.
SdreClip never posts content that does not belong to the connected account owner, and it does not use TikTok data for advertising or share it with third parties. Details: Privacy Policy.
