Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

This policy explains what information SdreClip accesses through the TikTok API and how that information is used. SdreClip is a personal application used by a single account holder to publish clips of their own live streams.

1. Information accessed

Within the granted scopes, SdreClip accesses the basic profile information of the authorizing TikTok account (user.info.basic: open id, display name, avatar) and the permission to upload video to that same account (video.publish). No data belonging to other TikTok users is accessed, collected or stored.

2. How information is used

Profile information is used only to identify the target account and to show the result of an upload. The publish permission is used only to upload video files produced from the account holder's own live streams. Information is not used for advertising, profiling or any purpose the account holder has not approved.

3. Sharing and sale of data

TikTok user data is never sold, rented or shared with third parties for advertising. It is not transferred to independent third parties except where required by law.

4. Storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored only for as long as the application needs them, on an access-restricted private server, and are never published. Video files are deleted from the server after upload. Reasonable technical and administrative measures are applied against unauthorized access, disclosure or alteration.

5. Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok account settings; revoking it invalidates the stored token. To request deletion of stored data, write to spikersorai@gmail.com.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated when the application or legal requirements change. The current version and its date are published on this page.

Türkçe özet

Başka TikTok kullanıcılarına ait hiçbir veri toplanmaz. Veriler reklam veya profilleme amacıyla kullanılmaz, satılmaz, üçüncü taraflarla paylaşılmaz. OAuth erişim bilgileri erişimi sınırlı özel bir sunucuda, yalnızca gerekli olduğu sürece saklanır; video dosyaları yükleme sonrası silinir. Yetki, TikTok hesap ayarlarından istenildiği zaman kaldırılabilir; verilerin silinmesi için spikersorai@gmail.com adresine yazılabilir.
