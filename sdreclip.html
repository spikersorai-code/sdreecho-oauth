Your stream highlights, ready for TikTok

SdreClip is a publishing tool built for the Sdre gaming channel. It takes highlight clips from the channel's own Kick live streams, turns them into vertical short videos with captions, and publishes them to the channel's own TikTok account through the official TikTok Content Posting API.

🎬 From stream to short

Long live streams are cut into 20–60 second highlights and reformatted to 9:16 with the facecam on top and gameplay below.

💬 Word-by-word captions

Speech is transcribed and burned in as readable captions, so clips work even with sound off.

📤 Official posting

Videos are sent with TikTok's Direct Post flow after the account owner signs in with Login Kit and grants permission.

🔒 Owner-only

SdreClip only publishes content the channel owner created, to the account the owner connected. It does not read, follow, like or message other users.

Who it is for

SdreClip is operated by the Sdre channel for its own content. It is not a public sign-up service: there is no account creation on this website and no third-party creator accounts are managed.
