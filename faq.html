Frequently asked questions

What does SdreClip do?

It turns highlights from the Sdre channel's own Kick live streams into vertical, captioned short videos and publishes them to the channel's own TikTok account.

Can other creators sign up?

No. SdreClip is operated for the Sdre channel's own content. There is no public registration or login on this website.

Which TikTok data does SdreClip access?

Only basic profile information of the connected account (to confirm which account is connected) and the permission to publish videos. It does not read other users' data, messages, followers or analytics.

Where are videos stored?

Clips are prepared on SdreClip's own server and uploaded to TikTok. They are not sold or shared with third parties.

How can access be removed?

Open TikTok → Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions, and remove SdreClip. Stored tokens are then deleted. You can also email us to request deletion.

Does SdreClip post copyrighted material?

SdreClip only uses the Sdre channel's own stream recordings.
